News

  • Nov 2026
    Kalpit (our M.Tech. alumni from 2024-26 batch) won the prestigious INAE Innovative Student Projects Award 2026. The award recognises his M.Tech research titled “Structure–Transport–Performance Coupling in Anisotropic Hydrogels for Passive Cooling Applications,” exploring the potential of anisotropic hydrogels for sustainable passive cooling. The research was conducted under the supervision of Prof. Chirodeep Bakli, with research mentorship from Dr. Vinay Arya in TENSoRE Lab IIT KGP.
